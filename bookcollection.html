Over the years at UIUC, I got a few books to support my education. Some of them are from giveaways, some of them I bought myself, some of them I borrowed from the library for a really long time. This page is dedicated to be a record of the books I've had here.

I may make some reviews or even personal diaries of things I've learned by reading them; you can click the hyperlinks on the book titles to get more details of my experiences with each one!
